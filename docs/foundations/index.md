# Foundations

> Every AI agent system bumps against three formal results. We don't
> try to engineer past them; we engineer **with** them. This section
> states each, sketches why it holds, and connects each one to a
> concrete piece of the ADK.

- **[Halting Problem](three-mathematical-limits.md)** — Turing, 1936. Why every agent loop needs an explicit budget.
- **[Rice's Theorem](three-mathematical-limits.md)** — Rice, 1953. Why evals replace formal verification.
- **[No Free Lunch](three-mathematical-limits.md)** — Wolpert & Macready, 1997. Why specialists beat generalists.
